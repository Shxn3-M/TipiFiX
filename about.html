About Us

We are a tech business located in Lusaka, Zambia that specialises in electronic diagnosis and repair. At the moment we are mainly focused on gaming consoles and accessories, but in future we will expand our repertoire and look at more devices.

Our Story

Gaming should be about playing, not about worrying whether your console will power on or your controller will start drifting halfway through a match. When something does go wrong, we think you should have a local team that can work out what the problem is and fix it properly.

That's why we set up shop in Lusaka. We started with the devices our customers needed help with the most, gaming consoles and their accessories, and we've built our skills and our stock of parts around them.

What We Do

Our work starts with diagnosis. Before we replace anything we find out what is actually wrong, so you only pay to fix the real problem.

  • Electronic Diagnosis

    Faults traced to the component level, from power problems to display and connection issues.

  • Console Repair

    Repairs for gaming consoles that won't start, overheat, or have stopped working the way they should.

  • Controller Repair

    Stick drift fixed with TMR or hall effect joystick replacements, worn grips swapped out, and unresponsive buttons restored with new conductive films.

  • Parts & Upgrades

    Quality replacement parts for sale if you'd rather do the repair yourself. Have a look at our products page.

How It Works

We keep the repair process simple and clear from start to finish.

  1. Get in touch

    Tell us what device you have and what's going wrong, or bring it straight to us in Lusaka.

  2. We diagnose

    We inspect and test your device to find the cause of the fault.

  3. You approve

    We explain what's wrong and what the repair involves, and we only go ahead once you're happy.

  4. We repair and test

    We carry out the repair, then test the device to make sure the problem is gone.

  5. Back to you

    You collect your device, ready to play again.
